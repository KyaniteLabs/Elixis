# Elixis design preflight

This receipt ties the title, the stone and the purpose together before the mark lands. It follows the `kyanite-product-design-preflight` skill. A choice this file can't back is marked as an assumption.

## 1. Purpose

Elixis is a local-first pattern synthesis engine. It turns raw references, repositories and folders into Source Corpora, Market Kits and identity kits.

- Outcome for the user: Users get a reusable synthesis of what makes their sources work, without sending them to a hosted service.

## 2. Title, stone and purpose

| Layer | Content | Evidence class |
|---|---|---|
| Title | Elixis: an elixir distilled from many sources | The product README |
| Stone | Labradorite (feldspar with labradorescence) for the body, citrine (quartz) for the perceptron nodes | The owner's brief, 2026-11-06 and 2026-11-10 |
| Product fit | A layered feldspar that flashes colour from inside stands for synthesis out of many layers. The perceptron makes the learning engine visible. | Our metaphor, approved by the owner at the eye-gate |

## 3. Rejected metaphors

- Glass flask or potion bottle (literal 'elixir' pun).
- A smooth gradient into citrine (rejected 2026-10-27: "citrine needs to be rawer darker less shiny").
- An opaque light ground behind the mark (must be transparent).

## 4. Material reference

Labradorite: dark grey feldspar with blue-gold labradorescent flashes. Citrine: honey-gold quartz, rendered rough and low-gloss rather than as faceted gemstone.

The landed render is `branding/elixis.png`: Labradorite shell with a criss-cross perceptron lattice (4, 5, then 2 nodes) in raw, low-gloss citrine, inside an open, daylit chamber.

## 5. Family law and form

- The icon must read as an icon: one compact, bold silhouette that holds at 32 px and 16 px. No scene, pedestal, text, rays or glow.
- The real mineral shows in broad regions.
- Family palette: the crystal material itself, turquoise `#00FFE6` edges, magenta `#FF1A6E` and yellow `#FFD11A` catches, white speculars, dark inner planes, transparent background.
- Form: An open labradorite chamber, lit by daylight through its open face, holding a 4-5-2 perceptron whose citrine nodes are fully connected layer to layer.
- Approval: the owner's eye-gate (2026-11-10). A concept SVG is never the icon; the mark is a pipeline render.

## 6. Typography

- Wordmark: **ELIXIS** in Chakra Petch Bold 700, uppercase, unmodified glyphs, native kerning.
- Endorsement: "by KyaniteLabs" in Chakra Petch Medium 500.
- The OFL licence ships with every export. On a dark canvas, invert only the ink.
